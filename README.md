# Flick Demon · School Desk Battles (React Edition)

School desk pen flicking battle game with custom pen studio, physics, AI opponents, and local ranks.

## Features
- **Pen Battle Physics**: Pull back and flick pens across a classic wooden school desk with collision detection, spin, and rubber-band aiming.
- **Custom Pen Studio**: Design custom pens with adjustable length, weight, flick power, color, and nib styles.
- **AI & 2-Player Modes**: Play against AI with three difficulty levels or hand the device to a friend in 2-player mode.
- **Stats & Ranks**: Track your wins, match history, pen levels, and leaderboards stored locally.
- **Immersive Audio & Haptics**: Web Audio API sound synthesis and tactile vibration feedback.
